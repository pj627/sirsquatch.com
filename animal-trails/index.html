The campers have wandered off, and their snacks are up for grabs. Time for a raid!

Draw a trail from any animal to sneak it into the campsite that matches its backpack, and don't let two of them bump into each other. Collect a moose, red fox, raccoon, beaver and skunk to unlock the legendary Sasquatch, or try Hard mode, where the campers are back with flashlights and every critter has to stay out of sight.

No ads. No tracking. No data collected. Animal Trails plays fully offline on iPhone and iPad.
