Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: Animal Trails does not collect, store, share or sell any of your data, and it does not track you.

This policy explains how the Animal Trails app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”), made by Paul Sanson (“we”), handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins and no online features.

Information kept on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a few things only on your own device: your best scores, your chosen animal, your game mode, and your sound and vibration settings. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

The app does not track you across other apps or websites. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party code that collects data. Because nothing is tracked, the app never asks for tracking permission.

In-app purchases

Animals are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple account information. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
